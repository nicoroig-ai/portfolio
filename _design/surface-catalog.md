# Surface brief: catalogue (home and project pages)

Modes: home is Experience, project pages are Read. Audience, job and constraints are in PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio read as a shelf of technical data books, where each system is a volume with a part number. It refuses the hero, three cards and skills list that this category always ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper and black ink under four saturated volume colours that own full-width fields. Archivo at heavy compressed and expanded widths. Outlined part plates, thick section rules, spec tables, decision rows.

STORY: The visitor sees four results at once, opens the one that matters to them, reads the result and spec, and goes as deep into the decisions as they want. Contact is there when they want it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top band with the name at 6rem on the left, and the intro sentence with Email and GitHub on the right. Below it, four full-bleed spines fill the viewport to the bottom edge, each with plate, compressed title, result sentence and domain, status and word count. Thickness is proportional to the word count. The primary action is opening a spine. Signature interaction: a spine slides out on hover and opens into its cover.

FORM: Data book, the first of the options shown and the one Nico chose. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
