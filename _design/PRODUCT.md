# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and a small script, published by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://nicoroig.dev. No build step. Stack choice was delegated, and picked so that a page stays editable without tooling.

## Users

People who follow a link to Nico's work, mostly from LinkedIn or GitHub, usually on a laptop or phone during the working day. Technical readers judge the reasoning; non-technical readers need the result from the first screen.

## Product Purpose

A growing public catalogue of systems Nico has built, added to slowly over years. Each entry shows what the system achieved and how it was reasoned through. Success: a visitor understands one result within seconds and can go as deep as they want.

## Positioning

Every entry documents how a system works and where it breaks: the options, the choice, the cost, the incident and the known gaps. A generated portfolio cannot copy this, because it comes from real production decisions.

## Operating Context

Nico confirmed: focus the site on the project and its result, not on what he wants to get out of it. So no job-seeking message and no sales pitch; contact is present and quiet.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Client and employer names are omitted deliberately.
- Client systems have no public code or demo; extracted pieces are public on GitHub.
- The English case-study text is Nico's own and is kept verbatim.
- The CV is never published (see `.gitignore`).

## Brand Commitments

Name: Nico Roig. Descriptor: AI engineer (assumption, set by the build; used to tell him apart from a musician who owns nicoroig.com).

## Evidence on Hand

Three written case studies (a fourth, cognitive training, was removed at Nico's request on 2026-11-09); the public repositories meta-webhooks, phone-identity, supabase-multitenant-rls and architecture-notes. No testimonials, client names, metrics or screenshots of the systems exist on the site, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The result comes first; the reasoning is one scroll away.
2. State the confidence you actually have, including failures and gaps.
3. Nothing on the page that the author cannot defend in an interview.
4. Adding an entry must stay cheap, or the catalogue stops growing.
